# M11 Food screens: plan

Requirements: `docs/PRD-1.2.md` (FR-NAV-2, FR-DIARY-*, FR-FOOD-*, FR-TGT-*, FR-NUT-*, FR-COACH-*, FR-FAST-1, FR-GAME-1). Design: the approved mockups in `design/screens-v2/food/` (F01–F07, X01), direction A minimal, Paper and Iron. Engines and data come from M10.

**Scope change against the M10 plan (D114):** M11 builds every Food screen, including the Review tab (F06) with the combined insights and their fixes, because the engines are ready and a Food section without its Review tab would not be shippable. M12 keeps: food XP in the shared level, the adaptive-target prompt, saved-meal management, polish, and the 1.2.0 release.

**Exit criteria**
- The `LiftLog | Food` switch on LiftLog Home (X01) and on the Food tabs; the app reopens in the last section. No other LiftLog screen changes.
- A real day can be logged in under 2 minutes: search (or scan), pick a portion, add; edit and delete entries; copy a meal.
- Targets, Review and Fasting work from real data; every number traces to an engine.
- Screens match the mockups in both themes at 360 and 430 px; axe clean; offline after the library import.
- Typecheck, lint, unit and e2e pass; budgets hold (first-load JS < 250 KB; Food code is lazy).
