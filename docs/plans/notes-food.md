# Notes from the Food area (M14, FR-DSN-11)

Date for all entries: 2026-11-10. Format: gap, decision, why. For the lead to move into `DECISIONS.md`.

1. **Diary header (F01).** The mockup has only a round "+" in the header and drops the day arrows, the streak, the quest and the Targets link. Decision: the title is "Food" with two buttons on the right (Targets, then the round "+" = Log food); the day arrows sit in the hero card next to the date; the streak follows "… kcal eaten"; the quest is a line at the bottom of the hero card. There is no bottom "Log food" button in this look. Why: Pranav reviewed this mockup, and the handoff (§8.1) says dropped features go in the nearest quiet spot.
2. **Diary bars (F01).** Colours: protein = accent, carbs = ink, fat = `--ll-fat`, fibre = `--ll-sage`, as in the mockup. Today's Food card uses `--ll-ink2` for fibre; the lead may want to make the two match.
3. **Add food (F02).** The mockup's "Done" in the header is not built (the back arrow does the same). The row shows the portion and protein under the name and the calories on the right. The tabs are a scrolling row of glass pills; the library notices sit in a glass card. The "kcal left · protein to go" pill from the mockup is built and sticks to the bottom; it shows only when targets exist. The classic "Not in the list? Create food" line is replaced by the "Create a new food" button, which still carries the typed name.
4. **Portion sheet (F03).** Amount first, then unit pills, then four numbers in a row, as in the mockup. The "Protein after this" bar is kept under them.
5. **Create food (F04).** The mockup has one card and the kind pills under it. Decision: pills on top (they change the form), then cards with the fields two to a row. Brand, serving name and grams, "Add more nutrients", recipe and quick add are all kept. The Save button is at the end of the form, not fixed, because a fixed button without a backing strip let the fields show through behind it.
6. **Targets (F05).** The mockup lists Fibre as an editable row. Fibre is not editable in the app (it follows calories), so the row shows the number with "follows calories" and opens the Micronutrients sheet. The mockup's "Reset to coach's pick" button is built and resets all edited macros (same as the classic "Reset"). The disclaimer sits at the foot of the inputs card so no text is on the sun.
7. **Review (F06).** Week / Month are two pills (the classic screen has one toggle link); previous / next are two round buttons beside them. The calories card draws one bar per day (height = share of the calorie target, capped at 120%; on target = accent, off = `--ll-fat`, nothing logged = a short track-coloured stub). Each bar has a text label ("Tuesday, Nov 10: 29% of the calorie target"), so the list doubles as the chart's table view. The mockup's "daily average in grams" rows are shown as share of target, which is what the review engine provides.
8. **Fasting (F07).** The mockup shows an "Eating window" countdown and a "Custom" plan; the app has neither, so they are not built. The ring shows the running fast (or "No fast running." with the plan pills). The week strip, the training line and "Change" are kept in a second card.
9. **Scanner.** Only shapes and type change. Seen while checking, not changed: with no camera (desktop, headless) in the dark theme the page behind the video is light, because the screen uses `bg-ink` and ink is light in dark themes. The classic look does the same. A fixed dark token would fix both looks; that is a shared-file change.
10. **Echo (FR-DSN-8).** `src/screens/food/echo.ts` `logWithEcho(date, targets, log)` wraps every place that adds diary entries: Add food, Create food (save and log), Quick add, and "Copy to today" on a meal. It reads the day's totals before and after, so nothing is stored. Editing or deleting an entry does not ripple. In the classic look it only runs the write.
11. **Shared kit.** `src/screens/food/ui.tsx` parts (`SegBar`, `Label`, `BigNumber`, `FoodHeader`, `TextTabs`, `Choices`, `RoundStepper`, `FieldRow`, `TextLink`) now branch on the look, so the sheets (height, activity, goal, macro, change fast, all nutrients, ingredient picker) take the new shapes without their own layouts. `.sun-pill` gets `aria-selected` / `aria-checked` states in `sun-food.css`; the lead may want to move that rule into `sun.css`.
12. **Toast position.** The toast covers part of the display title on Fasting for a moment ("Fast started"). Shared component, not changed.
