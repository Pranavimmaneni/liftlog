## M1 report

**Built**
- Seed script: 410 exercises from a pinned free-exercise-db snapshot, with derived patterns, load types, requirements, machine links and limitation areas. `SEED-REPORT.md` lists every drop and rename (FR-LIB-1, FR-LIB-2).
- Engines, all pure and tested: units / kg⇄lb rounding, load & volume, e1RM, prescription (level × goal × role × nudge), warm-ups, goal sets and bonus, rest timer, rotation, weeks, splits, auto-suggest, search, and the full goal + progression replay (FR-GOAL-1…5, FR-PRG-1…6) with in-session nudges (FR-WRK-9).
- `pictos.py` ported 1:1: all 10 Tier 1 illustrations, thumbnails and muscle maps are string-identical to the Python output. Colours are theme tokens (FR-ILL-1).
- Screens:
  - Onboarding S01–S03, then S04 with an auto-built routine (drag and keyboard reorder, swap, add). Resumes where you left off (FR-ONB-1…6).
  - S05 Home, S06 workout, S07/S08 logger (steppers with a keypad, warm-ups, effort chips, Hurts, edit/delete set, unilateral L/R, extra set, swap), S25 rest timer with alert, S09 ticket (totals, stamp, PRs, vs last, next time).
  - S13 library (search, filters, recent first), S14 settings (units, rest, warm-ups, alert, week start, profile, gym, backup/restore/CSV, typed delete).
  - First versions of S15 Plan and S12 History, plus S20 weigh-in.
- `/dev/fixture`: realistic 6-week demo data generated through the app's own engines.

**Phone checks for Pranav** (see PHONE-CHECKS.md)
1. Fresh install: onboard and log your first set in under 3 minutes.
2. Log a whole real workout. The goal meter fills, the rest timer counts down, and the ticket shows totals.
3. Mid-exercise, swipe the app away and reopen it. You land back in the session with every set intact.
4. Back up, delete the app, reinstall, restore. Everything is back.
5. Check the dumbbell exercise says "kg / hand" everywhere.
