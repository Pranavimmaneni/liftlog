## M13–M16 report: Rising Sun look

Built:
- Look switch, classic by default and unchanged; four token themes (workout / food × light / dark); self-hosted Dela Gothic One and Hanken Grotesk; the sun behind every screen except the scanners; frosted glass with an opaque fallback and a "Frosted glass" switch (FR-DSN-1, FR-DSN-2, FR-DSN-3, FR-DSN-9).
- Today, Active workout, Logging a set and the Rest timer on the logging screen (FR-DSN-4, FR-DSN-5, FR-DSN-6, FR-DSN-7).
- Echo motion: ripple on a logged set, food or weigh-in; celebration on a record, a food goal or a finished workout; once per goal per day; merged when several fire; a 2 s toast with reduced motion (FR-DSN-8).
- Every other LiftLog screen: onboarding, routine builder, Plan, summary, weekly review, strength levels, Progress, library, exercise detail, custom exercise, Settings (gym, profile, data), past session, body stats, weigh-in, install, scan and stickers, swap and target sheets, empty and delete states, system screens (FR-DSN-10).
- Every Food screen: Diary with fibre as a fourth bar, Add food, Portion, Create food, Targets, Review, Fasting, Meal, Scan (FR-DSN-11).

Deviations:
- Most mockups are first drafts (handoff §8.2). Where one drops or invents a feature, the existing behaviour was kept; the calls are in D130–D134 and D138–D141, with the full lists in `docs/plans/notes-{food,progress,plan,setup}.md`.
- Muscle maps keep their existing colours and pictograms are the app's own, as the handoff text says, although the mockups draw them differently (D129).
- Tab labels are 13 px, not the mockups' 11 px (D132).
- M14, M15 and M16 were built in one pass at Pranav's request instead of one milestone at a time (D135).

Decisions logged: 19 (D124–D142). The important ones: the new look is opt-in and classic stays the default until approved (D124); "reached" for food goals follows the 90% rule for calories and protein (D137).

Tests: 572 unit tests in 50 files, all passing. End to end: 180 tests on Pixel 7 and iPhone 14 in light and dark; the last full run had 177 passing and 3 failing in the new all-screens check, and after the two fixes the Rising Sun spec passed 16 of 16 (the other 164 were not rerun after that CSS-only fix). Axe is clean on 18 main routes plus Today, workout, logging, rest and Settings in the new look. First-load size budget OK; precache 3.17 MB of 5 MB.

Deployed: see PROGRESS.md.

Phone checks for Pranav: `docs/PROMPTS.md`, "M13–M16: Rising Sun".

- `S17: custom exercise…` (an older test) and a food search speed test time out now and then when the machine is busy; both pass alone and in a clean run.
- Not seen in a screenshot: UpdateToast, StorageBanner, Add food suggestions, the catalogue-progress notice, Targets without a weigh-in, "Copy to today", the "Swap into…" two-step sheet, a few dark-theme empty states.
- Scanner without a camera in the dark theme shows a light page behind the message (food notes, item 9).
- The Android APK was not built or smoke-tested with the new look.
- Food palette and ring colours are still open with Pranav (handoff §8.5); they are tokens, so they change in one place.
