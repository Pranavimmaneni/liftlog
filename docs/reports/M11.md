## M11 report: Food screens

Built:
- **Two sections (FR-NAV-2):** a `LiftLog | Food` switch at the top of LiftLog Home and of the Food tabs. It is the only change on LiftLog screens (mockup X01). Food has its own tab bar (Diary, Review, Fasting). The app opens in the section that you used last.
- **Diary, Add food, Portion, Meal (FR-DIARY-1..3, FR-FOOD-3, FR-NUT-1, FR-GAME-1):** calories left, macro bars, the protein quest, the 4 meals, and previous/next day. Search over the library and your own foods, with tabs (All, Recent, My foods, Indian, Brands). The portion sheet has units (katori, cup, grams), a stepper, kcal and macros, and "Protein after this". Each meal screen can edit, delete and copy its items.
- **New food and Scan (FR-FOOD-4/5):** your own food (label values per serving, stored per 100 g, empty = unknown), recipes (ingredients, cooked weight, servings) and quick add. The barcode scan uses the same lazy decoder as the QR scan, with EAN/UPC formats; a miss offers "Create food" with the number filled in.
- **Targets (FR-TGT-1..4, FR-TGT-6):** calories with the reason, macros (tap to change, with "Reset to coach's pick"), weight, height, activity, goal and rate, the 8 micros, and training-day carbs.
- **Review and Fasting (FR-NUT-2, FR-COACH-1/2, FR-FAST-1):** weekly and monthly review with days on target, macro shares, "Needs work" and "Going well" from food and training data, one-tap fixes, and low micros. The fasting timer has presets, a ring, the time left, "eat from", the week and a note about your training time.

Deviations:
- No mockup existed for the meal screen. It uses the F01/F02 parts, and the Diary got previous/next-day buttons (D117).
- On Targets, activity, goal, height and micros open sheets from their rows instead of showing all choices on the screen (D119).
- Review cannot yet say "losing too fast while strength drops", because strength change is not connected; training volume ignores the ×2 for dumbbells (D118).
- Scope: the Review tab and the coach moved from M12 into M11 (D114).

Decisions logged: 6 (D114–D119). Important: D114 (scope) and D116 (a day counts as a training day once a LiftLog session starts on it).
