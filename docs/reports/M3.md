## M3 report

**Built**
- **Deload (FR-PRG-7).** A deload week comes after every N normal weeks, where N depends on level (intermediate 6, advanced 5; beginners never). It also comes the week after 3 or more exercises stall at once, at least 3 weeks after the last deload (D53). A banner announces it the week before, on Home and on the Plan tab. Starting the workout then gives 60% of the usual sets.
- **S24 override sheet (FR-PRG-8).** Tapping the Goal card on the logger (S07/S08) lets you change sets, rep range and weight. The sheet shows the coach's pick and its reason. "Use my target" saves an override and records a `goalChange` with `overridden: true`. "Reset to coach's pick" removes it. The card then reads "Your target".
- **"Next time" line.** Once an exercise's sets are done, the logger shows the next target and its reason, for example "Next time: 102.5 kg × 10. Up 2.5 kg: every set hit 10."
- **S15 Plan tab, complete.**
  - Week strip (done vs planned) and the deload banner.
  - The queue can be reordered by dragging the handle, or with the arrow keys on the handle for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  - "Skip to" any session, and tap a name to edit that session (S04).
  - "Change split" runs S03 → S04 again. It archives the old routine and keeps all history (FR-RTN-6).
- **S16 exercise detail.**
  - The animated pictogram, or the muscle map when there is none, plus main/helper muscles and an in-gym chip.
  - Add to a session, "Swap into…" a session, and "Add to today's workout" while a session is open.
  - Instructions, plus history: best set, an e1RM chart with a "Show as table" view, and the last 5 sessions.
- **S17 custom exercise.** Create, edit and delete, with inline validation: the name is required and must be unique, and main muscle and load type are required. Deleting keeps the logged sets and removes the exercise from routines.
- **Tier 2 pictograms (FR-ILL-2).**
  - 219 exercises, drawn from 64 movement archetypes in `src/data/poses.json` by one generic renderer (`src/illustration/tier2.ts`). The format is logged as D55.
  - The pose data loads with the library chunk (33 KB), so initial JS is unchanged. Together with Tier 1 that makes 229 pictograms.
  - Animation now redraws only the moving figure, at about 30 fps.
- **`/dev/poses` gallery** with a filter and an animation toggle.
  - Contact sheets for review are in `docs/reports/poses/` (`tier2-paper-1…4.png`, `tier2-iron-1…4.png`).
  - Screens are in `docs/reports/screens/m3/`.
- **Delivered earlier and verified here:** every §5.8 rule with its reason (FR-PRG-1…6, unit tests since M1), goalChanges at finish, bonus-rep tracking, and pose animation.

**Performance note:** logging a set gives feedback in about 17 ms in Chromium. Playwright's WebKit on Windows takes about 250 ms, all of it IndexedDB. PRD NFR-PERF-2 asks for under 100 ms, so it is a phone check on a real iPhone. If a real iPhone is also slow, the fix is optimistic UI in the logger.
